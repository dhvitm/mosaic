## Valuation Methodology

- Cross-check with EV/EBITDA and P/E multiples

## Key Metrics & Benchmarks
- Revenue CAGR: Industry-dependent, typically 10-20% for growth companies
- EBITDA Margin: Varies by industry (10-15% manufacturing, 20-40% software)
- ROCE: Should exceed cost of capital (12-15% for Indian companies)
- FCF Conversion: 70-90% of EBITDA is healthy
- Debt/EBITDA: <3x is comfortable, >4x needs attention
- Interest Coverage: >3x minimum

## Risk Factors
- Revenue concentration (top customers, geographies)
- Margin pressure from competition
- Working capital intensity
- Currency exposure (for exporters)
- Raw material price volatility
- Regulatory changes
- Technology disruption
- Management quality and governance

## Modeling Notes

- Adjust for extraordinary items in historical data
- Normalize margins for one-time costs
- Consider seasonality in quarterly analysis
- Watch for related party transactions
- Verify auditor qualifications and remarks
